# SpotifyCares Intent Taxonomy

The intent taxonomy was derived from a sample of historical SpotifyCares
customer-support conversations from the Customer Support on Twitter dataset.

## Intents

### 1. Account & Security
Login, password reset, account recovery, hacked/stolen accounts,
and other account-access or security issues.

### 2. Subscription & Billing
Premium plans, subscription status, upgrades, payment issues,
charges, refunds, and related billing questions.

### 3. Playback & Streaming
Problems with playing audio, pausing, buffering, shuffle/repeat,
playback controls, or streaming connections.

### 4. App & Web Player Technical
Technical problems with the Spotify application or web player,
including crashes, freezes, loading problems, browser issues,
cache/cookie issues, and client-specific errors.

### 5. Content Availability
Questions or complaints about missing or unavailable songs,
albums, artists, regional restrictions, or licensing.

### 6. Playlist, Library & Sync
Problems involving playlists, saved music, downloads,
library management, or synchronization across devices.

### 7. Features & Product Feedback
Feature requests, product suggestions, UX feedback, and complaints
about existing product functionality.

### 8. Other / Unclear
Messages that cannot be confidently assigned to another intent.
